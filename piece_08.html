Five watercolor prints mapping the lunar cycle from new moon to full and back again. The lunar cycle has long been used as a framework for intention — the new moon as a time to begin, the full moon as a time to release.

This bedroom was designed with rest and renewal in mind. The moon phases are a quiet reminder that every month offers a fresh start.
