Duealberi is a creative studio based in Rome, run by two artists — Antonia and Fabio — specializing in contemporary abstract art inspired by playful abstract expressionism. The name means “two trees” in Italian. Each print is fine art giclée, signed and numbered on archival paper.

These three pieces connect three chapters: Italy, South Africa, and a day at the University of the Witwatersrand (Wits) in Johannesburg, where a South African mentee and I each chose our favorite piece from a student show. The soft sage greens and blue-grey washes echo an abstract work loved that day that was never tracked down.

The bright yellow frames were added later — a deliberate choice to bring more light and energy into the space.
